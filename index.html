Privacy Policy

App: DesiSwagTunes
Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what information the DesiSwagTunes app ("we", "the app") collects when you log in with Facebook or connect a Facebook Page or Instagram account, and how that information is used.

1. Information we collect

  • Your public Facebook profile (name, profile picture, user ID) when you log in with Facebook.
  • Details of the Facebook Pages and Instagram business accounts you choose to connect, such as page name, page ID and access tokens.
  • Content you choose to publish through the app, such as images, videos and captions.

2. How we use it

  • To log you in and identify your account.
  • To publish and schedule posts to the Pages and Instagram accounts you connect.
  • To show you basic results for those posts.

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Storage and security

Data is stored only as long as needed to provide the service and is protected with reasonable technical safeguards. Access tokens are kept private and never shared.

4. Data deletion

You can delete your data at any time in either of these ways:

  1. Go to Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, find DesiSwagTunes, and click Remove. This revokes the app's access.
  2. Email us at tklakhanpal@rediffmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". We will delete all data linked to your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

5. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
